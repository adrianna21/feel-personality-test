ABOUT: FEEL

What is FEEL?

FEEL is a 12-question story-based personality quiz that maps your emotional instincts across four axes — how you express, process, decide, and move through the world. Each result is one of 16 personalities, each with its own emotional signature.

Made by MA(A)AD Studios
